# Spotta AI Knowledge Base

## What Spotta Does
- Enter a destination, get ranked nearby parking.
- Ranked by distance, price, and availability confidence.
- Runs in the browser, no app download needed.
- Coverage: downtown Vancouver only, for now.

## Parking Data
- Real City of Vancouver meter locations and posted rates.
- Distance is measured from your searched destination.
- Price is the real posted hourly meter rate.

## Confidence Score
- Shown as: Likely open, Uncertain, or Often full.
- Computed fresh for every search, not a fixed number.
- Based on time of day/day of week, and price relative to nearby spots.
- It is an estimate, not a live guarantee.
- Always check posted signage on arrival.

## Map and Navigation
- Results shown as pins around your destination.
- Selecting a spot opens Google Maps directions to the real location.

## Accounts
- Sign up or log in to track spots you have visited.
- Login is required to see your saved activity.

## FAQs
- Q: Is a spot guaranteed to be open? A: No, confidence is an estimate.
- Q: Do I need an account? A: Only to save and view visited spots.
- Q: How is confidence calculated? A: Time of day and price versus nearby spots, not live sensors.
