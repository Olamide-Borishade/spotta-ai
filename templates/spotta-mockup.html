01 · The problem

Every drive downtown ends with the same ten minutes of guessing.

Drivers waste time, fuel, and patience circling for parking in busy urban areas — especially downtown Vancouver, where availability shifts block by block.

TIME LOST
~10 min
Typical search time per trip in a dense downtown core before finding an open spot.
FUEL BURNED
Idle miles
Circling the block adds unnecessary driving with no destination — pure waste.
PATIENCE
Every trip
The stress compounds daily for commuters, visitors, and delivery drivers alike.
02 · The solution

One recommendation, built from every signal that matters.

Spotta runs entirely in the browser and blends approved data sources into a single best recommendation — updated as conditions change.

Input
Parking data

Approved municipal and operator feeds on availability and restrictions.

Input
Historical patterns

Time-of-day and day-of-week trends learned from past occupancy.

Input
Traffic conditions

Live congestion folded into the route-and-park recommendation.

Input
User reports

Crowdsourced confirmations that keep predictions honest in real time.

Input
Computer vision

Permitted vision sources add ground-truth signal where available.

04 · Business model

Free to start. Built to scale beyond drivers.

Freemium consumer

Core search and recommendations free for every driver; premium subscription unlocks predictive alerts and priority routing.

B2B analytics

Venues and operators get occupancy and demand insights to price and manage their lots more effectively.

Municipal partnerships

Future data-sharing agreements with cities to improve curb management and enforcement at scale.

05 · Go-to-market

Vancouver first. Then city by city.

Stage 1

Launch the MVP in Vancouver

Prove the recommendation engine in one dense, high-demand downtown core before expanding.

Stage 2

Partner locally

Work with churches, businesses, and parking operators who already feel the parking pinch on peak days.

Stage 3

Expand city by city

Take the same playbook — approved data, local partners, MVP first — into the next urban market.

Mission

Help people spend less time searching for parking and more time getting where they need to be.
