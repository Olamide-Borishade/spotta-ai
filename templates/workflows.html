How Spotta's components work together

Find Parking Near a Destination

Uses: Destination Geocoding, Ranking Engine, Ranked Results + Map

Geocode the typed destination, rank nearby spots by distance, price, and availability confidence, then show them as a list and on the map.

💬

Ask the Assistant About Parking

Uses: AI Assistant, Knowledge Base

A user asks a question in the chat, and the assistant answers from the Spotta knowledge base, no lookup required.

🔁 agentic

Find Me a Better Spot

Uses: AI Agent, Ranking Engine, Destination Geocoding

Take a goal (destination, duration, budget), search and evaluate the results, then iteratively relax constraints until an acceptable spot is found, explaining the tradeoffs made along the way.
